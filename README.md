# American Dhammapada

*Walking the American Way*: the stories of the Dhammapada commentary (*Dhammapada-aṭṭhakathā*), retold one at a time across American history, from 1686 to today.

**Read it:** https://rec3141.github.io/american-dhammapada/

- Browse by commentary number, by year, or on the map.
- Each story ends with a Background section: the Dhammapada verse in F. Max Müller's 1881 translation, a summary of the original commentary story, and the history behind the setting.
- Stories are written by Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.2 under a shared house style (`tools/HOUSE_STYLE.md`), with a shared registry of symbols and cross-references (`tools/MOTIFS.md`) and an editor's seed list (`tools/SEEDS.md`).
- The original commentary stories are linked from each page, via [Ancient Buddhist Texts](https://ancient-buddhist-texts.net/English-Texts/Dhamma-Verses-Comm/index.htm).

All scripture quoted is from public-domain translations.
