# Seeds from the editor

Images, places and worlds the editor wants in the series. Before you choose a setting, check this list. If a seed fits your source story, use it and add your story number next to it. A seed can be used more than once, but its first use should be a strong one.

- Video games (arcades, LAN parties, speedrunners, streamers, MMO guilds, a dad who plays at night) [3.3]
- Quakers (beyond 1750s Newport: Underground Railroad, conscientious objectors, a modern unprogrammed meeting)
- Detainees (ICE detention, Japanese internment beyond Heart Mountain, Guantánamo, an Angel Island interrogation)
- The Minnesota Twins (the 1987 or 1991 Series, the Metrodome, a radio in a barn)
- Salt Spring Island, British Columbia (back-to-the-landers, draft dodgers, the Black settlers of the 1850s, Hawaiian Kanaka families) [25.9]
- A windmill (a Plains water pump, a Dutch mill in Michigan, a wind farm in Texas or Iowa) [12.9]
- Boring white people (suburbs, office parks, bowling leagues, mainline Protestants, middle managers, the ordinary Midwest)
