How instruction words move Qwen3-TTS pitch

Qwen3-TTS CustomVoice 1.7B, one fixed sentence (“She put the bucket down and scooped up two handfuls and held them out to him.”), three seeds per condition. Pitch is the median F0 (Praat), in semitones relative to the same speaker with no instruction. Click any dot to hear that take.

Which words move pitch

Each row is an instruction. Dots are the mean of three seeds; the thin line spans the seed range. The shaded band is seed noise (±2 SE of a 3-seed mean): shifts inside it are indistinguishable from re-rolling the seed.

Dose words

“Speak with a slightly / much / extremely higher (lower) pitch.”

Steering the instruction embedding

Neutral instruction, with α × (high-pitch words − low-pitch words) added to every instruction-token embedding. A causal test of a linear “pitch direction”. Shift is relative to α = 0.

Does the static embedding predict the shift?

x: how far each instruction's mean token embedding lies along the pitch direction (relative to the neutral instruction). y: measured shift, averaged over speakers.
